How-to guides

1Find any document in 30 secondsThe fastest paths to anything in the library.
  1. Press / and type a few words — the search looks across all documents.
  2. Or use the filter chips (Policies, Templates, Easy Read…) to narrow by type.
  3. Or browse: 01 NDIS is organised by topic — everything about a subject lives in one outcome folder.
  4. Still stuck? Open the Master Register (button above) and search the spreadsheet.
2Check you have the current versionOld copies go stale silently — here's how to be sure.
  1. Current documents carry a version and date in the name, e.g. “V2.0 (10-7-2026)”.
  2. Anything marked ARCHIVE, or inside an _ARCHIVE folder, is history — never use it.
  3. In Splose, current form templates end in “V2.0 (2026)”.
  4. If two copies disagree, the one in this drive wins — and tell Trent or Alex.
3New here? Your first hourThe orientation path for new starters.
  1. Read the READ ME (button above) — five minutes, the whole system.
  2. Open 01 NDIS and skim the NDIS Staff Handbook at the top of the folder.
  3. Find your discipline's essentials: treatment plan template (O20), service agreements (O21), incident policy (O16).
  4. Bookmark this hub — press / anytime you need something.
  5. Questions are welcome: Trent or Alex, or the team meeting.
4Something's wrong or missingThe library stays trustworthy because people speak up.
  1. Don't work around it — flag it. Message Trent or Alex with the document name.
  2. If it's urgent for a participant matter, ask before proceeding rather than guessing.
  3. Spotted an out-of-date detail (a rate, a name, a phone number)? Same — flag it, it gets fixed at the source.
  4. You will never be criticised for raising a problem. Sitting on one is the only wrong move.
5Splose or the drive?Two homes, two jobs — here's the line.
  1. Doing daily work with a participant (referrals, agreements, plans)? Use Splose — templates ending “V2.0 (2026)”.
  2. Reading the rules, printing a blank master, or sending an Easy Read? Use this drive.
  3. Client files and clinical records never live in this drive — Splose and the Disciplines drive only.
  4. Live registers (incidents, complaints, RP) are in the Confidential drive — restricted on purpose.
