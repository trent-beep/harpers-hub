## Snapshot

Amanda Robinson, aged 14, was last seen on Saturday 21 April 1979 walking along
Lake Road, Swansea (Lake Macquarie, NSW), after attending a dance at her high
school in Gateshead — about 400 metres from her home, and thirteen days after
Robyn Hickie disappeared in the same district. (Sources: NSW Police reward
page, accessed Aug 2026; AFP NMPCC profile; Newcastle Herald, "Decades of
suspended grief for loved ones")

A physical description was not reproducible from retrievable allowed-source
text (UNVERIFIED here); her photograph and description appear on the NSW Police
reward page and AFP profile.

## Investigation history

- 1979 — Original investigation subject to the coroner's later criticisms:
  "ad hoc, unstructured and dysfunctional"; failures to consider links between
  the cases, to take alibi statements, to obtain written statements from
  important witnesses, and to follow up leads — prompting a NSW Police apology
  to the families. (Sources: Newcastle Herald, "Fresh inquiry…", 2025; NSW
  Parliament Submission 63)
- c. 1998 — Within Strike Force Fenwick's re-investigation. (Sources: NSW
  Parliament Q&A 4450; Submission 63)
- 2001–2002 — Joint coronial inquest before State Coroner John Abernethy;
  findings July 2002: deceased, most likely as a result of foul play — murdered
  by persons unknown. (Sources: NSW Police reward page; NSW Parliament
  Submission 63)
- 2019-04 — Strike Force Arapaima re-examination (with Hickie and Kotevski).
  (Sources: NSW Police reward page; ABC News, 25 Nov 2019)
- 2019-11 — Green mid-1970s Holden Torana publicly named a "significant"
  possible lead for the Hickie and Robinson cases. (Sources: ABC News,
  25 Nov 2019; Newcastle Herald, Nov 2019)
- 2021-08-16 — $1,000,000 reward announced. (Sources: ABC News, 16 Aug 2021;
  NSW Police reward page)
- 2021-08 — Arapaima search at Nords Wharf (former Camp Kanangra). (Source:
  Newcastle Herald, Aug 2021)
- 2025-10 — Era raised at the NSW Legislative Council inquiry into unsolved
  murders and long-term missing persons 1965–2010. (Source: NSW Parliament
  inquiry page)

## What police ask the public

- $1,000,000 reward on offer for information regarding the disappearance and
  suspected murder. (Source: NSW Police reward page)
- Specific standing ask: information about the green mid-1970s four-door Holden
  Torana sedan. (Sources: ABC News, 25 Nov 2019; Newcastle Herald, Nov 2019)
- Anyone with information: **Crime Stoppers 1800 333 000** or the Crime
  Stoppers online reporting page. (Source: NSW Police reward page)

## Family advocacy

- Anne Robinson (mother) has spoken publicly through the Newcastle Herald
  appealing for information. (Source: Newcastle Herald, "Mums' plea over
  mystery of missing girls")

## Open questions on the record

1. The precise gap in her route — "somewhere between the Pacific Highway and
   Lake Road, Swansea" — is how the record itself frames the unknown. (Source:
   Newcastle Herald, "'I hope to God they get answers'")
2. The green Torana remains a police-named open lead. (Source: ABC News,
