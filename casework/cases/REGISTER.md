# Hunter Region register sweep — candidate index

Officially-listed long-term missing persons cases (missing > ~2 years)
connected to Newcastle, Lake Macquarie and the wider Hunter (Maitland,
Cessnock, Port Stephens, Upper Hunter), NSW. Compiled 2026-08-26 from the AFP
National Missing Persons register, NSW Police reward/appeal pages, and
reputable coverage of official appeals. This is a landscape index, not deep
case research; cases marked ▶ have an opened case file in this directory.

Per a 2020 Newcastle Herald review of the AFP register, at least 29 people were
then unaccounted for across the Hunter and Central Coast.

**Access note:** the register sites (missingpersons.gov.au, police.nsw.gov.au,
newcastleherald.com.au) could not be fetched directly from the compile
environment; entries were built from search-indexed content of those official
pages plus fetch-accessible official and news sources. Verify URLs on next
sweep.

**Cultural note:** entry 15 concerns an Aboriginal / Torres Strait Islander
man; Charter rule 6 (cultural protocols) applies to any material drawn from
that entry.

## Official processes and regional initiatives

- **NSW Police Missing Persons Registry** (est. Jul 2019, State Crime Command) —
  reviews all missing person reports back to 1945; ~1% become long-term.
  https://www.police.nsw.gov.au/can_you_help_us/missing_persons/missing_persons_registry/missing_persons_registry
- **Missing Person Enquiry System** (searchable): https://www.police.nsw.gov.au/can_you_help_us/missing_persons/missing_persons_registry/missing_person_enquiry_system
- **Family DNA collection drive (2022)** — Newcastle was one of four NSW pop-up
  sites collecting relatives' DNA for the Registry's identification database.
  (Herald)
- **National Missing Persons Week** (AFP NMPCC, annual, first week of August) —
  Hunter cases profiled locally each campaign.
- **Re-investigation strike forces** — Arapaima (2019: Hickie, Robinson,
  Kotevski), Bambil (2025: Kotevski, with NSW Crime Commission), Kummari
  (Fenwick).
