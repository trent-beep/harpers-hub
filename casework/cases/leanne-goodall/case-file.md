## Snapshot

Leanne Beth Goodall, aged 20, was last seen on 30 December 1978. Around 1pm her
brother dropped her at Muswellbrook Railway Station; she was planning to attend
Newcastle Technical College and was excited by the prospect. She never arrived
at her intended destination. (Source: AFP NMPCC profile, accessed Aug 2026) The
Newcastle Herald records her as last seen alive at the Star Hotel, Newcastle,
that day. (Source: Newcastle Herald, "Decades of suspended grief for loved
ones")

A physical description was not reproducible from retrievable allowed-source
text (UNVERIFIED here); her photograph and description appear on the official
AFP profile.

## Timeline

- 1978-12-30 ~13:00 — Dropped at Muswellbrook Railway Station by her brother,
  intending travel toward Newcastle (Technical College plans). [OFFICIAL]
  (Source: AFP NMPCC profile)
- 1978-12-30 — Last seen alive at the Star Hotel, Newcastle. [REPORTED]
  (Source: Newcastle Herald, "Decades of suspended grief for loved ones")
- Never seen since; never located. [OFFICIAL] (Source: AFP NMPCC profile)

The two last-seen accounts (Muswellbrook station; Star Hotel Newcastle later
the same day) are recorded side by side as the record gives them; this file
does not resolve them.

## Investigation history

- 1978–2000s — At the inquest it was established that **no formal criminal
  investigation was ever launched** into her disappearance. The coroner
  questioned the retired officer formerly in charge of Newcastle district
  detectives about why no detective examined the case; he answered: "I don't
  know. It should have been." (Source: NSW Parliament Submission 63)
- c. 1998 — Within the scope of Strike Force Fenwick's re-investigation of
  greater-Newcastle disappearances. (Sources: NSW Parliament Q&A 4450;
  Submission 63)
- 2001–2002 — Joint coronial inquest (with Robyn Hickie and Amanda Robinson)
  before State Coroner John Abernethy at Toronto NSW; findings July 2002:
  murdered by persons unknown. See the linked record for the inquest's findings
  on investigative failures. (Sources: NSW Parliament Submission 63; Newcastle
  Herald story 495179, headline withheld)
- 2025-10 — Cases of this era raised at the NSW Legislative Council inquiry
  into unsolved murders and long-term missing persons 1965–2010. (Source: NSW
  Parliament inquiry page)

## What police ask the public

- Anyone with information: **Crime Stoppers 1800 333 000** or the Crime
  Stoppers online reporting page. (Source: AFP NMPCC profile)
- A $100,000 reward is listed for this case on the NSW Police government
  rewards schedule (offence date 30/12/1978). (Source: NSW Police GIPA release
  2020-129)

## Family advocacy

- Her mother, Mrs Leen, through the Newcastle Herald: "We are desperate, that
  is what we are. We need to know, you have to come forward." (Source:
  Newcastle Herald, "Mums' plea over mystery of missing girls")

## Open questions on the record

1. Why the case received no formal criminal investigation — raised and left
   unanswered at the inquest itself ("I don't know. It should have been").
   (Source: NSW Parliament Submission 63)
2. Whether the Goodall reward remains at $100,000 while the two linked cases
   were raised to $1M in 2021 — the record reviewed shows no upgrade and no
   stated reason. (Sources: NSW Police GIPA 2020-129; ABC News, 16 Aug 2021)
