03 · The two who knew

Anonymous callers police are still seeking

Live police ask · Jun 2026

Mon 5 Dec 1994 · ~6:50pm
A man telephoned Newcastle Police Station — eleven days after the abduction — giving "specific information about a person's involvement in the matter".

Live police ask · Jun 2026

Wed 18 Sep 1996 · ~9:25pm
A man called radio station KOFM Newcastle and spoke with a presenter, likewise giving specific information.

What the record itself establishes: within eleven days, and again in 1996, knowledge of a person's involvement existed in this community — held by at least two local men who each chose a phone over silence, and anonymity over a name. Strike Force Bambil's 2026 appeal asks them to come forward. That appeal — not any private theory — is the thread the public can pull: Crime Stoppers 1800 333 000.
