# Gordana Kotevski

**Missing from Charlestown, NSW, since Thursday 24 November 1994.**

**A $1,000,000 reward is on offer. If you know anything, call Crime Stoppers on
1800 333 000 or report at https://nsw.crimestoppers.com.au. Information is
managed confidentially.**

## Who Gordana is

Gordana Kotevski was 16. On the evening of Thursday 24 November 1994 she was
shopping with friends at Charlestown Square, in the Lake Macquarie area near
Newcastle, during Thursday late-night trading. The Australian Federal Police's
national missing persons profile remembers her as "a bubbly teenager who loved
her family and was adored by her friends".

Her aunt, Julie Talevski, said at the 2022 reward announcement: "Gordana was
young, joyous, innocent, and then she was gone."

## What happened, from the official record

About 9pm that night, Gordana was walking from Charlestown Square to her aunt's
home on Powell Street, Charlestown, when she was forced into a vehicle.
Witnesses nearby reported hearing a struggle between Gordana and at least two
males before a white ute was seen leaving the area; the AFP's national profile
records a white 4WD seen in the area at the time.

Despite extensive investigations at the time and since, Gordana has never been
located. In 2003, the NSW State Coroner found that Gordana was deceased, most
likely as a result of foul play.

## The $1,000,000 reward

The NSW Government offers a reward of $1,000,000 for information leading to the
arrest and conviction of the person or persons responsible for Gordana's
disappearance and suspected murder. The reward was announced on 25 May 2022 —
with Gordana's mother, Peggy Kotevski, present — increasing the earlier
$100,000 reward. It remains on offer today.

## A direct appeal: two anonymous callers, 1994 and 1996

In June 2026, Strike Force Bambil investigators made a specific public appeal
for two men who each contacted authorities anonymously with specific
information about a person's involvement in the matter, and who have never been
identified:

1. **About 6.50pm on Monday 5 December 1994**, a man telephoned **Newcastle
   Police Station**.
2. **About 9.25pm on Wednesday 18 September 1996**, a man called radio station
   **KOFM Newcastle** and spoke with a presenter.

Police urge both callers to come forward and speak with investigators about the
information they hold, and urge anyone with knowledge of what happened to
Gordana — in Lake Macquarie, the Hunter, or elsewhere — to contact Crime
Stoppers on 1800 333 000.

## The investigation is active

Since early 2025, Strike Force Bambil — Lake Macquarie Police District
detectives assisted by the NSW Crime Commission — has been re-examining
Gordana's disappearance and suspected murder. In April 2026, investigators
searched several properties at Edgeworth and West Wallsend and seized items for
forensic examination. In May 2026, police renewed their public appeal, and
excavation work was reported underway at a Lake Macquarie site.

## Her community has never let go

In November 2023, days before the 29th anniversary, graffiti remembering
Gordana appeared on buildings in the Newcastle CBD. Detective Sergeant Kristi
Faber described it as "a way of someone in the Hunter community reminding
everyone that we must never forget this crime".

From that moment, Gordana's family and the Hunter community built something
lasting: over about 16 months they raised more than $22,000, and in July 2025 a
26-metre mural of Gordana was unveiled at Cardiff Shopping Centre. More than
three decades on, her face is still part of the place she came from.

## About this page

This page is maintained by a small public-record support project. We are not
police, not detectives, and not a tip line; we claim no authority and hold no
information beyond the public record. Everything above comes from official
police and government sources and published news reporting, listed below.
Anyone who contacts this project with information about the case will be
directed to Crime Stoppers, immediately and verbatim.

The page is refreshed around 24 November (the anniversary of Gordana's
disappearance) and during National Missing Persons Week (the first week of
August).
