## Snapshot

Gordana Kotevski, aged 16, was last seen about 9pm on Thursday 24 November 1994
on Powell Street, Charlestown (Lake Macquarie / Newcastle, NSW), being forced
into a vehicle while walking from Charlestown Square Shopping Centre to her
aunt's home on the same street. (Source: NSW Police Force reward page, accessed
Aug 2026) The AFP National Missing Persons Coordination Centre profile records a
white 4WD seen in the area at the time. (Source: NMPCC profile, accessed Aug 2026)

A detailed physical description (height/hair/eyes) is not published in the
official appeal text reviewed — the appeals circulate her photograph. The NMPCC
describes her as "a bubbly teenager who loved her family and was adored by her
friends". (Source: AFP NMPCC media release, 25 May 2022)

## Timeline

- 1994-11-24 (evening) — Gordana, 16, was shopping with friends at Charlestown
  Square during Thursday late-night trading. [OFFICIAL] (Source: NSW Police
  National Missing Persons Week release, Aug 2020, via ABC News NSW briefing,
  6 Aug 2020)
- 1994-11-24 ~21:00 — Last seen being forced into a vehicle on Powell Street,
  Charlestown, while walking from Charlestown Square to her aunt's home on the
  same road. [OFFICIAL] (Source: NSW Police reward page)
- 1994-11-24 ~21:00 — Witnesses nearby reported hearing a struggle between
  Gordana and at least two males, before a white ute was observed leaving the
  area. [OFFICIAL] (Source: NSW Police reward page)
- Vehicle description variance across the record — NSW Police reward page: "white
  ute"; NMPCC profile: "white 4WD"; ABC (24 Nov 2023): police have previously
  looked into a white single-cab Toyota HiLux with a silver work tray.
  [DISPUTED — recorded, not resolved here] (Sources: NSW Police reward page;
  NMPCC profile; ABC News, 24 Nov 2023)
- 1994-12-05 18:50 — An anonymous man phoned Newcastle Police Station providing
  "specific information about a person's involvement in the matter"; police are
  still seeking this caller (2026). [OFFICIAL] (Source: NSW Police news article,
  2026; Newcastle Herald, Jun 2026)
- 1996-09-18 21:25 — An anonymous man called radio station KOFM Newcastle and
  spoke with a presenter, likewise providing specific information; police are
  still seeking this caller (2026). [OFFICIAL] (Source: NSW Police news article,
  2026; Newcastle Herald, Jun 2026)
- Despite extensive investigations at the time and since, Gordana has never been
  located. [OFFICIAL] (Source: NSW Police reward page)

## Investigation history

- 1994 — Initial NSW Police investigation following the Powell Street abduction;
  extensive inquiries at the time. (Source: NSW Police reward page)
- 2003 — Coronial inquest: then NSW State Coroner John Abernethy found Gordana
  was deceased, most likely as a result of foul play. (Source: NSW Police reward
  page)
- Pre-2012 — $100,000 reward announced ("more than 10 years" before the 2022
  increase; exact year not located — UNVERIFIED beyond ABC's phrasing).
  (Source: ABC News, 25 May 2022)
- 2019-04 — Strike Force Arapaima established by Lake Macquarie detectives to
  re-examine the disappearances of Robyn Hickie (18, missing 1979), Amanda
  Robinson (14, missing 1979) and Gordana Kotevski. (Source: ABC Newcastle
  radio, 2019; ABC News, 24 Nov 2023)
- 2020-08 — Renewed appeal during National Missing Persons Week, 25+ years on.
  (Source: ABC News, 6 Aug 2020)
- 2022-05-25 — Reward increased to $1,000,000; announced at Belmont Police
  Station with Gordana's mother present. ABC reported five detectives on the
  case, led by Detective Sergeant Kristi Faber. (Source: ABC News, 25 May 2022;
  NSW Police reward page)
- 2023-11 — Days before the 29th anniversary, graffiti about Gordana appeared on
  Newcastle CBD buildings; Det Sgt Faber said it was heartening to see her
  memory living on, "a way of someone in the Hunter community reminding everyone
  that we must never forget this crime". (Source: ABC News, 24 Nov 2023)
- 2025 (early) — Strike Force Bambil formed: Lake Macquarie Police District
  detectives assisted by the NSW Crime Commission, re-examining the
  disappearance and suspected murder. (Source: NSW Police reward page; ABC News,
  4 May 2026)
- 2026-04 — Bambil investigators searched several properties at Edgeworth and
  West Wallsend, seizing items for forensic examination. (Source: ABC News,
  4 May 2026; Newcastle Herald, Apr 2026)
- 2026-05 — Renewed public appeal; police flagged possible use of an excavator,
  and excavation work was subsequently reported underway at a Lake Macquarie
  site. (Source: ABC News, 4 May 2026; NSW Police news article, 2026; Newcastle
  Herald, May 2026)
- 2026-06 — Strike Force Bambil publicly appealed for the two anonymous callers
  (5 Dec 1994, Newcastle Police Station; 18 Sep 1996, KOFM) to come forward.
  (Source: NSW Police news article, 2026; Newcastle Herald, Jun 2026)

## What police ask the public

- The $1,000,000 NSW Government reward remains on offer for information leading
  to the arrest and conviction of the person or persons responsible. (Source:
  NSW Police reward page)
- Anyone with information: **Crime Stoppers 1800 333 000** or
  https://nsw.crimestoppers.com.au — information managed confidentially.
  (Source: NSW Police reward page)
- 2026 appeal: anyone with knowledge — in Lake Macquarie, the Hunter, or
  elsewhere — is urged to come forward. (Source: NSW Police news article, 2026)
- Specifically: the anonymous caller to Newcastle Police Station (~6.50pm,
  Mon 5 Dec 1994) and the anonymous caller to KOFM (~9.25pm, Wed 18 Sep 1996)
  are urged to speak with investigators about the information they hold.
  (Source: NSW Police news article, 2026; Newcastle Herald, Jun 2026)

## Family advocacy

- Peggy Kotevski (mother) — present at the 2022 reward announcement; in May 2026
  said the new developments brought "a little bit of relief but also anger".
  (Sources: ABC News, 25 May 2022; ABC News, 4 May 2026)
- Julie Talevski (aunt) — at the 2022 announcement: "Gordana was young, joyous,
  innocent, and then she was gone". (Source: ABC News, 25 May 2022)
- AFP NMPCC video (International Missing Children's Day, 25 May 2022) featuring
  Peggy, sister Carolina and aunt Julie, on ambiguous loss and a permanent
  commemoration site. (Source: AFP NMPCC media release, 25 May 2022)
- 2023–2025 — Community mural campaign begun after Julie Talevski's son Matt saw
  the 2023 graffiti: ~16 months, $22,000+ raised, a 26-metre mural of Gordana
  unveiled at Cardiff Shopping Centre in July 2025. (Sources: ABC News,
  18 Jul 2025; Newcastle Herald, 2024 and 2025)

## Open questions on the record

Only questions the record itself raises:

1. The two anonymous callers (1994, 1996) each held "specific information about
   a person's involvement" and have never been identified — police's own
   standing ask. (Source: NSW Police news article, 2026)
2. The vehicle description varies across official artifacts (ute / 4WD / a
   specific HiLux configuration previously examined) — the record does not
   reconcile these. (Sources: NSW Police reward page; NMPCC profile; ABC News,
   24 Nov 2023)
